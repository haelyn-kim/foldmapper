ProteinMPNN
https://github.com/dauparas/ProteinMPNN

The following portions of FoldMapper are adapted from ProteinMPNN:

  foldmapper/model_utils.py
      - ProteinFeatures, EncLayer, DecLayer, PositionWiseFeedForward,
        PositionalEncodings
      - gather_edges, gather_nodes, gather_nodes_t, cat_neighbors_nodes
      - featurize (modified to carry an evolutionary-profile tensor)
      - loss_nll, loss_smoothed
      - NoamOpt, get_std_opt

  foldmapper/utils.py
      - StructureDataset, StructureLoader, worker_init_fn
      - build_training_clusters
      - get_pdbs, PDB_dataset (extended to load PDB + TED shards and a
        cached per-residue evolutionary-profile tensor)
